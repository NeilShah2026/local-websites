Meet the Owner

Hi, I'm Phil.

I started Yaitanes Landscaping in 1986, and I'm still the one who walks your yard, gives you the price, and shows up with the crew.

Anybody can cut grass. What I like is the plant side of it — figuring out why a tree is struggling and fixing it, instead of just trimming around the problem.

"Take care of the soil and prune it right, and the yard mostly takes care of itself."

I've been working on yards around Stoughton long enough to have watched trees I planted grow up. Give me a call and I'll come take a look.
