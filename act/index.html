◆  How It Goes

Four steps, no surprises

Every job runs the same way, whether it's twelve tiles or twelve thousand square feet.

STEP 01

Walkthrough

We come out, get up on a ladder and look at what's actually there — grid condition, tile size, what's above it.

STEP 02

Takeoff & Quote

A written number with the material spec named on it. No allowances that balloon later.

STEP 03

Schedule

Nights, weekends or phased by floor if you can't shut the space down. We work around your building.

STEP 04

Install & Punch

Clean install, dust controlled, debris hauled out. We walk it with you before we call it done.
